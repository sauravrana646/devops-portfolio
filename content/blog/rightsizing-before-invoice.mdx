Cluster spend grows quietly when requests are folklore and idle nodes have no owner. The fix is rarely a new cost tool—it is accountability plus a latency budget.

## Baseline before you consolidate

Capture cost by namespace and the SLIs that matter. If you cannot say what “too slow” means, you will “optimize” into an incident.

## Fix requests, then reclaim capacity

Right-size the loudest services first. Only then widen consolidation windows. Karpenter (or your autoscaler of choice) amplifies good requests and punishes bad ones.

## Show teams their own waste

Finance dashboards that only platform reads do not change behavior. Namespace attribution turns FinOps into a product conversation—the same pattern that makes error budgets stick.
