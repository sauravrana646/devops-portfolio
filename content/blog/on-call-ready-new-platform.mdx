Shipping a platform capability without an on-call story exports risk to product teams. “On-call ready” is a checklist, not a vibe.

## Runnable runbooks beat slide decks

For each critical path: how do you detect it, who owns it, what is the first command, and what is the rollback. If the answer is “page the person who built it,” you are not ready.

## Default telemetry on the golden path

New services should inherit dashboards and alerts. Optional observability is optional until the outage.

## Practice the exception path

Break-glass and freeze procedures need a tabletop before production pain. The first time you use them should not be the first time you invent them.
