Internal developer portals fail when they become wallpaper over snowflake pipelines. The portal is not the product—the paved path is.

## Ship one boring path first

Pick the majority language and runtime. Template the repo, CI, GitOps bootstrap, secrets, and default observability. Measure time-to-first-prod-deploy. Improve that number before you invent a marketplace.

## Document escape hatches honestly

Teams will leave the path. Pretending otherwise creates shadow platforms. Write the approved escapes, the approval needed for privileged ones, and what support you will not provide.

## Make defaults excellent

Golden paths win on gravity: the easy way should also be the safe way. If the custom route is faster and safer, your paved road is not paved yet.

For delivery, that often means running what secure CI/CD already signed—instead of rebuilding the app on the platform. See [One golden path for trusted software](/blog/consume-signed-images-not-rebuild/).
