A warning in CI is not security. If risky changes still reach a release, and nobody can prove what shipped, you have theater—not a supply chain.

## Make the path obvious

Every change should take the same route: review, automated checks, then release. Critical findings stop the change. There is no quiet side door for “just this once.”

## Sign what you ship

A green build is not enough. Sign the release so deploy and admission can verify origin. Harden the container by default—non-root, slim base—so “safe” is what actually runs.

## Prove the gates

If checks never fail when they should, people stop believing them. Keep an honest example of a bad change that gets blocked. Trust follows what engineering and security can see.
