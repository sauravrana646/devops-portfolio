Error budgets fail quietly when they become an SRE dashboard nobody else reads. The point is not a prettier burn graph—it is a shared decision about when to ship and when to stabilize.

## Pick journeys, not machines

Start with a user-visible journey: checkout authorization, search results, file upload. Machine SLIs without product stakes turn into alert spam. Journey SLIs create a scoreboard leadership can argue with productively.

## Tie budgets to release policy

A budget without a freeze/unfreeze rule is vibes. Agree in writing: what happens at 50% burn, at 100%, and who can override. Then review monthly with the people who decide roadmap—not only the people who answer pages.

## Retire symptom alerts

Once burn-rate alerts exist, delete the noisy cousins. If an alert does not change a decision, it is training your team to ignore pain. The win is fewer pages and clearer conversations—not more panels.
